# Requested source-backed recipe expansion

This pack adds **15 recipes and 41 ingredients** to the existing common catalogue. It leaves the original 48-recipe/97-ingredient source snapshot intact. Existing and newly created households resolve the additions from the same `2026-11` starter release; household overrides, vetoes and historical recipe snapshots stay separate.

The user approved reference-weight estimates for imprecise quantities and one explicit adaptation of the roasted vegetables. Recipes retain their source links, original ingredient amounts, normalization weights and estimate flags. Methods are concise bilingual paraphrases, not copied articles. Source ratings are a dated snapshot from 8 November 2026, separate from household star ratings. User-supplied recipes are included regardless of whether their source publishes ratings.

## Nutrition and quantities

Twenty-seven new foods use the existing checksum-pinned UK CoFID 2021 workbook. Exact selected cells and factors are preserved in `requested-cofid-rows.json`. Fourteen additional foods use linked UK retailer/manufacturer product labels where CoFID lacks the required food/form or reports unknown energy. Unknown CoFID values are never treated as zero. Truvia's zero is an explicit label value, not a missing-data fallback.

Each ingredient retains the dataset or product URL. Brand-specific names identify product references that should be matched to the actual purchased product. A different product requires a household edit. Nutrition remains an estimate; recipe publishers' calorie totals are not substituted for the app's ingredient-based calculation.

Small weighed amounts below 1 g/ml now round to tenths, with a positive minimum of 0.1 g/ml. Larger amounts retain whole-unit/between-ten rounding, and pieces retain their defined conversion increments. This applies equally in the client and SQL meal-transfer calculation, preventing spices from making an otherwise valid recipe unsuggestible. Calories and shopping contributions use the actual rounded amount.

All imported recipe lines are normalized to grams for a complete source batch, and the original source quantities stay attached separately. One egg, a small onion, a handful, a can's drained content and similar measures use user-approved, editable estimates. They are not presented as measurements published by the recipe author. Estimates also cover optional finishing amounts and volume-to-weight conversions where density is not published. No cooking-yield conversion silently changes raw chicken into cooked chicken or dry beans into canned beans. Sun-dried tomato and bamboo-shoot weights are drained. The gnocchi label publishes 164 kcal per 100 g cooked and approximately 132 g cooked from 125 g uncooked. Its uncooked energy reference is explicitly normalized to 173.184 kcal/100 g using that published approximate yield, rather than applying cooked nutrition directly to the dry/as-sold weight.

The balsamic label is per 100 ml; its per-100-g energy is explicitly derived using the identified 1.06 g/ml density estimate. Other liquids use the identified normalized weights in the recipe record. The corn estimate counts edible kernels, excluding the cob. Baking recipes should be prepared as batches and divided after cooking; the app's personal scaling describes ingredient shares.

Original starter recipes retain their existing household-edit behavior. The additions introduce no new photos, remote image fetches, external translation calls or paid service.

## Rebuild and rollout

The reviewed source quantities and bilingual method summaries are in `tools/recipe_expansion/inputs.json`. Rebuild with the pinned workbook already used by the starter tooling:

```sh
python tools/recipe_expansion/build.py --workbook /path/to/cofid-2021.xlsx
```

The builder uses Python's standard library and the repository's existing Prettier installation. It writes `requested-recipes.json`, `requested-cofid-rows.json` and pending migration 22. It does not download source pages or execute SQL. Do not regenerate or replay an already applied migration when later editing the catalogue; publish a new additive migration instead.

Apply [migration 22](../../supabase/migrations/202611080022_sourced_recipes.sql) after successful migrations 1–21 and before deploying this frontend. Entries are additive to the shared starter table. Household-owned rows take precedence over shared IDs, and past snapshots are never rewritten. The household revision counter signals catalogue availability to connected clients.

No hosted SQL, deployment or merge is performed during implementation. See the PR for executed database, recipe-planning and bilingual browser checks.
