48 original bilingual reference recipes. Amounts are for one reference serving, before personal scaling and rounding.
Pesos para una ración de referencia, antes del ajuste personal y el redondeo.

Weights are raw edible/dry, or the explicitly stated ready-to-use product. Dry pulses need the prerequisite soaking/cooking shown below; finishing times do not include that preparation.
Los pesos son en crudo comestible/seco o del producto preparado indicado. Las legumbres secas requieren el remojo y la cocción previa que figuran en cada receta; los tiempos finales no incluyen esa preparación.

Calories are CoFID-based estimates, not laboratory analyses of finished dishes. Recipes are desk-validated, not kitchen-tested. Complete shared preparations once; main/side methods refer to those same quantities, not extra food. Intended dietary tags require matching product labels where specified. ## b01 — Apple and ginger porridge / Gachas de avena con manzana y jengibre

Base: **413.38 kcal**; with optional dessert / con postre opcional: **413.38 kcal**.
Hands-on / preparación activa: 5 min; finishing cook / cocción final: 8 min. Prerequisite cooking is additional.

Patterns without dessert / patrones sin postre: omnivore, pescatarian, vegetarian

### Apple and ginger porridge / Gachas de avena con manzana y jengibre

| Ingredient / Ingrediente | Quantity / Cantidad |
| --- | ---: |
| Porridge oats / Copos de avena | 50 g |
| Semi-skimmed milk / Leche semidesnatada | 200 g |
| Raw eating apple, skin on, core removed / Manzana cruda con piel, sin corazón | 120 g |
| Fresh ginger / Jengibre fresco | 2 g |
| Walnut kernels / Nueces sin cáscara | 10 g |

1. Core and dice the apple; grate the ginger. / Retira el corazón de la manzana, córtala y ralla el jengibre.
2. Simmer the oats, milk, apple and ginger gently for 5–8 minutes, stirring; add a little water if needed. / Cuece suavemente la avena, la leche, la manzana y el jengibre durante 5–8 minutos, removiendo; añade un poco de agua si hace falta.
3. Serve with the measured chopped walnuts. / Sirve con las nueces pesadas y troceadas.
